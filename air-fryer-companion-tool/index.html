Methodology

How the Air Fryer Companion works

Each tool uses evidence-based cooking science to give you accurate, personalised results every time.

1
Density Modifier

Food density affects heat penetration. Airy foods need less time; dense cuts need more. The Density Modifier calibrates both temp and time accordingly.

2
Wattage Science

Higher wattage = faster, hotter cooking. The Wattage Adjuster uses a proportional model to correct time and temperature for your exact machine's output.

3
Sync Scheduling

The Multi-Food Timer back-calculates each item's start time from the desired finish time, so every food is done at exactly the same moment.

4
Safe Temperatures

Meat doneness targets are based on USDA and UK FSA guidelines. Internal temperature targets are always shown alongside time — because time alone isn't safe.

5
Crunch Chemistry

Crispiness is a function of surface moisture, temperature, and time. The Crispiness Optimizer adjusts all three plus shake frequency to hit your exact crunch target.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the air fryer need a different temperature than an oven?
Air fryers circulate hot air at high speed in a small cavity, which transfers heat much more efficiently than a conventional oven. This means you typically need to reduce temperature by around 15–25°C (25–45°F) and reduce time by 20–30% to avoid overcooking.
How does wattage affect cooking?
A higher-wattage air fryer heats up faster and reaches target temperature more quickly. A 1200W model cooking the same food as a 2000W model may need 15–25% more time at slightly higher temperature to get the same result.
Is it safe to cook chicken wings from frozen?
Yes — the Frozen Food Converter is calibrated for this. For food safety, always ensure poultry reaches an internal temperature of at least 74°C (165°F), regardless of the time shown. Always use a probe thermometer for poultry.
Why do I need to shake or flip during cooking?
Air circulates from above in most air fryers, meaning the bottom of your food can cook more slowly. Shaking or flipping ensures even browning and crispiness on all sides, especially for fries, nuggets, and anything breaded.
How often should I clean my air fryer?
For light use, a quick wipe of the basket after each use and a proper wash every 3–5 uses is sufficient. For greasy foods like sausages, wings, or bacon, clean after every use. A deep clean of the interior and heating element should happen every 2–4 weeks depending on frequency.
Can I cook eggs in an air fryer?
Yes — try the "Can I Air Fry This?" tool above. Hard-boiled eggs (in shell) work very well at 130°C / 270°F for 15–17 minutes. Scrambled and poached eggs are not suitable. Fried eggs can work in a small heat-proof dish with a light oil spray.
